By Ella Light
(April 17)—Ashley Waycroft, CEO of
Belmont Developers, has announced plans
to purchase four strip malls in the greater
Minneapolis area. The storefronts of these
one-level malls will be repurposed for use
as apartments.
According to Waycroft, “As businesses in
this area leave malls in favor of online
stores, mall owners lose rent, and the city
loses valuable tax revenue. Combine these
issues with the city’s need for more
moderate-income housing, and this
venture has the perfect set of
circumstances to make everyone happy.”
Perhaps the biggest problem Belmont
faces is local zoning boards. To reach the
desired number of apartments, Belmont
needs to subdivide the stores. The zoning
requirements will vary by location.
Zoning laws in the suburb of Tansberg,
for example, require residential units to
be a minimum of 600 square feet.
This innovative venture could be an
exceptional asset as unused property will
be repurposed, and much-needed
affordable housing will become available.
