### Passage 1

#### Web Page

Orion Outdoor Club

Our charter requires inclusive, safe outings. Do not cause wanton trail damage or goad inexperienced members into risky behavior. Before entering the river, practice how to exhale calmly under guidance. No guide can prevent every accident. Follow instructions and stick to the marked route. Report a loose strap or tangle of ropes before departure. The leader may cancel an outing if conditions make the planned route unsafe. Separate rules apply to competitive events: athletes must not dope before a race, and officials may require testing.

### Passage 2

#### Email

To: Orion Trip Desk
From: Marco Lane

I booked May 5. Your leaflet says heavy rain could strand us beyond the bridge. I prefer postponement to an improvised crossing. This plea is urgent because I must arrange leave. When is the decision made, and can my booking transfer? I can ask my employer about a different date, but need notice rather than a change on arrival.

### Passage 3

#### Reply

Dear Mr. Lane,

The guide reviews conditions at 6 p.m. on May 4. If unsafe, we postpone to May 12 and transfer your booking unless you request a refund. We will not try an unplanned crossing. Members receive a message after the review. Keep both dates in mind when arranging leave. The same equipment requirements apply to the alternative date.

### Question 195

When will the decision be made?

- A. At the postponed outing
- B. The evening before the original outing
- C. Noon on May 12
- D. A week after May 5

### Answer

B

### Explanation

May 4 at six is the evening before May 5. The other times are incorrect.

### Question 196

What happens by default if the crossing is unsafe?

- A. A second payment
- B. Automatic transfer to May 12
- C. Cancellation without alternatives
- D. An unmarked crossing

### Answer

B

### Explanation

Transfer is automatic unless a refund is requested. The reply rejects improvised crossing and mentions no extra fee.

### Question 197

How does the reply reflect the charter?

- A. It guarantees no accidents
- B. It avoids unnecessary risk
- C. It encourages dares
- D. It allows ignoring the guide

### Answer

B

### Explanation

The refusal of an improvised crossing follows the safety principle. Neither a total guarantee nor risky behavior is promised.

### Passage 1

#### Article

Instrument Workshop Open Day

Legendary instrument maker Clara Verne welcomes visitors August 3. At her career's zenith she worked for major orchestras; now she trains apprentices. She shows how to tune worn instruments rather than promising all can be restored. A paltry repair budget can create false expectations, she warns. Inspect the wood before quoting a price. The open day includes consultations by appointment, not free repairs.

### Passage 2

#### Form

Client: Rosa Malik
Instrument: Violin
Requested date: August 3, afternoon
Problem: Loose fitting and unstable tuning
Budget: $120

Inspect before starting work. Contact me if costs exceed the limit; I am not seeking a replacement. I can leave the instrument for several days, but need a written estimate first. Please use email because I cannot answer calls during working hours.

### Passage 3

#### Reply

Dear Ms. Malik,

Your appointment is 2:30 on August 3. Inspection is $20 and remains part of the final bill if you proceed; it will not be charged twice. If the wood is sound, the fitting adjustment is a further $70, making $90 altogether. This is not guaranteed before inspection. In a similar vein, not every tuning problem can be solved in one visit. No repairs begin without your approval. We will email the estimate after inspection.

### Question 198

What does Ms. Malik want?

- A. A private concert
- B. A new instrument at any price
- C. Apprentice training
- D. Assessment and possible repair

### Answer

D

### Explanation

Her form identifies repairs, requests inspection, and excludes replacement. Training and a concert are not requested.

### Question 199

What is the expected total for the stated adjustment?

- A. $110
- B. $70
- C. $120
- D. $90

### Answer

D

### Explanation

$20 inspection plus a further $70 totals $90 as explicitly stated. The other figures omit, double-count, or use the budget limit.

### Question 200

What is necessary before repair begins?

- A. Increasing the budget to $200
- B. The client's approval
- C. Canceling the open day
- D. Buying a new violin

### Answer

B

### Explanation

The reply requires approval, matching the form. The other conditions are not required.
