# Part 6

## Passage Group 1

### Type

Email

### Passage

**To:** All Harbor Street staff  
**From:** Facilities Office  
**Subject:** Lobby renovation

Work on the lobby will begin Monday. The main entrance will be closed, so employees should use the east entrance. Temporary signs will be posted to guide visitors. **[1]** Please allow extra time when arriving during the first week.

### Question 131

Work on the lobby will _____ on Monday.

- A. begins
- B. begin
- C. began
- D. beginning

### Answer

B

### Explanation

The modal verb “will” takes the base form **begin**.

### Question 132

Which sentence best fits at **[1]**?

- A. The cafeteria menu changes every month.
- B. The project was canceled last year.
- C. Security staff will also be available to provide directions.
- D. All visitors received a refund.

### Answer

C

### Explanation

The inserted sentence continues the topic of helping visitors navigate the temporary entrance.

### Question 133

Why should employees allow extra time?

- A. The usual entrance will be unavailable.
- B. A staff meeting begins early.
- C. Parking fees have increased.
- D. The east entrance is permanently closed.

### Answer

A

### Explanation

Employees must use a different entrance while the lobby is renovated.
