# Part 5

## Question 101

The company plans to _____ a new branch next year.

- A. opening
- B. opened
- C. open
- D. opens

### Answer

C

### Explanation

`plan to + base verb` is required, so **plan to open** is correct.

### Vocabulary

- branch: a local office of a larger organization
- plan to: intend to do something

### Tags

- grammar
- infinitive

## Question 102

Please submit the expense form _____ Friday afternoon.

- A. by
- B. among
- C. during
- D. beside

### Answer

A

### Explanation

**By Friday afternoon** means no later than that deadline.

### Tags

- preposition
- deadline

## Question 103

The marketing team responded _____ to the unexpected change in demand.

- A. flexibility
- B. flexible
- C. flexibly
- D. flex

### Answer

C

### Explanation

An adverb is needed to modify the verb “responded.”
