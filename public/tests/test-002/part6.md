**From:** Human Resources
**To:** All Staff
**Subject:** Response to the Flexible Start Time Petition

Dear Staff,

Thank you to everyone who signed the recent petition requesting more flexible morning start times. After reviewing the proposal with senior leadership, we are pleased to announce a three-month pilot program that will allow employees to _____ their start time anywhere between eight and ten in the morning, provided they still complete a full eight-hour shift. **[1]** Employees who wish to participate should confirm their preferred schedule with their direct supervisor by the end of this week. We will collect feedback throughout the pilot and decide whether to make the policy permanent based on how it affects both productivity and employee satisfaction.

We appreciate your patience as we worked through the logistics of this request, and we're glad the petition led to a solution that works for more of our staff.

Best regards,
Human Resources

### Question 131

Which word best completes the blank?

- A. select
- B. selecting
- C. selected
- D. selection

### Answer

A

### Explanation

The base verb form is required after the infinitive marker "to." "Selecting" and "selected" are verb forms that cannot follow "to" this way, and "selection" is a noun.

**Riverside Logistics — Warehouse Notice**

Please be advised that yesterday's shipment of electronic components from our overseas supplier has been placed under quarantine by the customs inspection team after a routine check identified irregularities in the packaging documentation. Until further notice, no one should open, move, or otherwise _____ any of the affected pallets, which are currently located in Bay 7. **[1]** We expect the inspection to be completed within three to five business days. If the shipment passes inspection, it will be released to the warehouse floor and processed as usual; if not, we will provide further instructions on returning or disposing of the goods.

We apologize for any disruption this may cause to your workflow and appreciate your cooperation.

Warehouse Operations Team

### Question 135

Which word best completes the blank?

- A. handle
- B. handling
- C. handled
- D. handles

### Answer

A

### Explanation

The blank must match the parallel base-verb structure of "open, move, or otherwise ___" following "should." The other forms break this parallel structure.

Clearwater Pharmaceutical announced Monday the opening of a new research facility in the city's innovation district, a project that took nearly three years to _____ complete. ### Question 139

Which word best completes the blank?

- A. fully
- B. full
- C. fullness
- D. fulfill

### Answer

A

### Explanation

An adverb is needed to modify the infinitive verb "complete." "Full" is an adjective, "fullness" is a noun, and "fulfill" is a different verb meaning to satisfy a requirement.

**To:** Sales Team
**From:** Client Relations
**Subject:** New Client Introduction — Bennett & Cole Architects

I'd like to introduce everyone to our newest client, Bennett & Cole Architects, who signed a two-year service agreement with us last week. Attached to this memo is a full client _____ that includes their company history, current projects, and key contacts within their organization. **[1]** Please take some time to review this information before our kickoff call on Thursday, since understanding their background will help us tailor our proposal to their specific needs. If you have any questions before then, feel free to reach out to me directly.

Thanks,
Client Relations Team

### Question 143

Which word best completes the blank?

- A. profile
- B. profiled
- C. profiling
- D. profiles

### Answer

A

### Explanation

A singular noun is needed after "a full client ___." "Profiled" and "profiling" are verb forms, and "profiles" is plural, which does not agree with the article "a."
