## Question 101

Employees are encouraged to submit their expense reports ______ to avoid delays in reimbursement.

- A. promptly
- B. prompt
- C. promptness
- D. prompting

### Answer

A

### Explanation

An adverb is needed to modify the verb "submit," describing how the reports should be submitted. "Prompt" is an adjective, "promptness" and "prompting" are nouns/gerunds, neither of which can modify a verb this way.

## Question 102

The consultant's recommendation was ______ on the assumption that raw material costs would remain stable.

- A. precedent
- B. predicated
- C. presumed
- D. persisted

### Answer

B

### Explanation

"Predicated on" means based on a stated assumption, which fits the sentence exactly. "Precedent" is a noun and cannot follow "was," "presumed" would require "that" rather than "on," and "persisted" means continued, which does not fit the meaning.

### Vocabulary

- predicate (verb): to base or found something on a stated assumption

## Question 111

The new safety regulations ______ across all branches by the end of next quarter.

- A. will implement
- B. are implementing
- C. will be implemented
- D. have implemented

### Answer

C

### Explanation

Regulations cannot implement themselves, so the passive voice is required: "will be implemented" shows that the regulations will receive the action. The active forms in A, B, and D incorrectly treat "regulations" as the one performing the action.
